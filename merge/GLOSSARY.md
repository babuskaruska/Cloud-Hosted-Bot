# Saarepagar glossary

All player-visible text is Estonian. This list keeps the terms the same everywhere. When you add text, use these words and follow the notes at the end.

## Tone

- Speak to the player as "sina": "Toksa ahju", "Said tähe!". Never "teie".
- Keep it warm, short and a little funny. Every character has a voice:
  - **Liisa** is chatty and lives on social media ("Panen kõik pildid üles!").
  - **Ants** speaks in very short sentences and starts with "Hm.".
  - **Peeter** is the lighthouse keeper. He writes poems and has a podcast with three listeners.
  - **Muri** is a cat. Lines are "Mjäu." or "Mrr." with the meaning in brackets: "Mjäu. (Ma ei tea midagi.)".
  - **Maali**'s lines are letters ("Vanaema Maali kiri").
  - **Mina** is the heroine. She is cheerful and modern.
- Task names are imperatives: "Paranda kai", "Riputa laternad". A party or festival task may be a noun: "Avapidu!", "Õunapidu".

## Places and people

- **majakas** (the story area, Majakas) and **tuletorn** are both fine. Use "majakas" in the story and "Tuletorni kaljud" only for the chapter.
- **Sadam**, **Aed** and **Tuuleveski** are the story areas, plus **Vanaema saladus**.
- Use the full name "Vanaema Maali" in titles and "vanaema" in sentences.

## Grammar notes

- After a number use the partitive singular: "3 münti", "5 käiku", "2 tähte", "1 käik".
- Write ordinals with a dot: "7. päeval", "15. samm", "3. saar".
- Quotes are „…“, the ellipsis is one character (…), and "×" is used for counts and combos.
- These loanwords are fine in a character's voice: podcast, smuuti, DJ, boho, Instagram. UI text has no English.
- Words never shrink below 15 px on screen. If a label does not fit, shorten the word rather than the font.
