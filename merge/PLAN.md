# Saarepagar version 3: the big update plan

The goal is endless, addictive, beautiful fun that women love and kids enjoy too. It must stay healthy, with no real-money purchases and no dark patterns. Everything is in correct, natural Estonian.

## 1. Endless content (never runs out)

- **Level map ("Seiklus"):** a winding, Candy Crush–style map of numbered levels that never ends.
  - Each level is a short puzzle on its own small board.
  - **Goals:** make 3 cakes; clear the ice from 8 tiles; deliver 5 orders; free the cat from boxes.
  - The player has a limited number of moves or energy, and earns 1–3 stars.
  - **Obstacles:** ice, boxes, cobwebs, locked crates, sand, and bubbles that spread.
  - **Boosters:** a magic wand (upgrades an item), scissors (splits one item), shuffle, +5 moves.
  - Levels are generated from the level number, so they are deterministic, get harder in waves (easy, easy, hard, then a breather) and never end.
  - Every 10th level is a boss level ("Suur tellimus"), and every 50 levels opens a new themed map chapter.
- **Endless island tour ("Saareringreis"):** after the 6 story areas, new islands are generated forever. Each has its own theme, tasks and decor: flower boutique, spa, ice-cream kiosk, fashion boutique, wedding garden, beach club, Christmas market, autumn orchard, and so on.

## 2. Addictive loops (healthy)

- **Lucky wheel ("Õnneratas"):** one free spin a day, plus spins earned in play.
- **Daily login calendar:** 7- and 30-day streaks with growing prizes.
- **Free season pass ("Hooaja rada"):** about 30 reward steps a month, filled by playing.
- **Weekly rotating events:** a garden party, a fashion week, a wedding, a beach festival. Each has its own currency, a special chain and a reward track.
- **Collections and achievements with badges.** A free piggy bank ("Hoiupõrsas") fills with coins from play and opens every day.
- **"Just one more" hooks:** a near-miss "only 1 move short!" offer of +5 moves bought with in-game coins; the next level button always in view; a streak bonus for winning levels in a row ("Võiduseeria").
- **Healthy limits:**
  - nothing costs real money;
  - no fake countdown pressure;
  - an optional "rest" reminder after a long session (useful for the kids).

## 3. Decorating and style (what many players love)

- **Design choices:** each restoration task offers 3 style choices (e.g. curtains: boho, minimal, romantic). The scene shows the choice, and it can be changed later.
- **Heroine wardrobe:** outfits, hairstyles and accessories unlocked by play.
- **Pets:** café cat Muri gets accessories, and new pets come later (puppy, bunny).
- **Photo mode:** a pretty "postcard" of the decorated area to screenshot and share.

## 4. More effects and juice

- Items wobble and sparkle when idle; merges chain in a rainbow trail; board-wide celebrations.
- The scene has weather and time of day (sunset, rain, snow in the winter event).
- Level-complete fireworks; a star-collect cascade on the map; a gentle phone vibration on merges (Android).

## 5. Language

- A full Estonian proofread of every string: spelling, grammar, case endings, natural tone, and consistent terms.
- Item names are checked for real Estonian words.
- A glossary goes in `merge/GLOSSARY.md`.
